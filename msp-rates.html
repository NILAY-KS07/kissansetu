What is MSP?

A simple explanation

Minimum Support Price (MSP) is a government announced reference price intended to provide farmers with price support for eligible crops. KissanSetu uses MSP information to help farmers understand the expected reference value of their produce before procurement.

Before you sell

Keep these things in mind

1
Check your crop

Make sure the crop and applicable season match the listed rate.

2
Check quantity

Procurement value depends on the quantity accepted at the centre.

3
Check quality requirements

Accepted produce may be subject to applicable quality standards.

i
Important

MSP figures displayed here are reference values for the application. Final procurement and payment records should always be based on the applicable government notification, procurement centre rules, accepted quantity and produce quality.
